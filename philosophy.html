Six things we are sure of

What changes in the next decade.

None of these are predictions about technology. They are about how companies will be run.

01

Software starts doing the work.

You used to pay for seats and do the work yourself. Now you pay for the job done.

02

Machines carry the repeated work.

Most office work is rules on repeat. Machines are good at it and never tire.

03

People move up.

When machines take the busywork, people do what only people can: judgement, taste and the hard call.

04

What you already know is the edge.

The technology is the same for everyone and changes every week. Your industry knowledge does not.

05

Chasing every new tool is a trap.

It is a full time job that pulls you off your real one. Go deep on what you know, we keep the technology current.

06

The org chart gets a second kind of member.

People and agents side by side, each with a clear owner. Someone runs the machines the way a good manager runs a team.
