How a plug-in is born

One client, one problem. Then every client after.

One client, one problem.

2

We found Polynome, cameras and microphones that read a site the way a foreman would, and plugged it into that client's cockpit.

3

It joins the menu.

A manufacturing client plugged the same thing into its floor next. Every client after inherits it.

Polynome runs the floor, the cockpit runs the company. Built by Polynome, sold through the cockpit, and the cockpit sold through Polynome. The deal runs both ways.
