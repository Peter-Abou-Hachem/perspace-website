Why the agent waits

Projects, not agents. The agent comes in once the work is fixed.

An agent pointed at a broken process only does the wrong work faster. So we fix the work first, prove it held, then hand the repeated part to a machine.

Proof it held

Checked at 30, 60 and 90 days.

Every change is measured against how the work ran before. A step counts as done when the agreed measure moves.
